# License, rights, privacy, and acquisition risk register

**Verified:** 2026-09-05  
**Status:** planning document; **not legal advice**. Legal counsel and institutional privacy/security review remain required.

## Interpretation

- **Legal fact** records what an official license, agreement, terms page, or rights record says or does not say.
- **Risk assessment** is the project's operational judgment; it is not a legal conclusion.
- Likelihood and impact use `Low / Medium / High / Critical` planning scales.
- A software or annotation license never silently extends to embedded or referenced third-party media.

## Release checklist

A release candidate cannot pass unless:

- the manifest validates against `acquisition_manifest_schema.json`;
- acquisition, processing, derivative-release, annotation-release, transcript-release, and raw-media-release decisions are all explicitly recorded;
- no applicable decision is `unknown_hold`;
- raw files enter the public package only when the raw-media release decision is `allowed`;
- exact license/grant/terms evidence, version, licensor or rightsholder, snapshot hash, reviewer, and decision date are preserved;
- required attribution/notices are generated and checked;
- privacy/safety review is complete;
- source and derived hashes, media time base, and preprocessing versions are present;
- restricted and public objects are in separate storage and build pipelines;
- a link-rot or withdrawal fallback is assigned for evaluation-critical items.
