## 3. Separation before semantics

### 3.1 Split hierarchy

The split freeze records three levels:

1. **Atomic source group.** The complete continuous source or coherent
   session and all clips, views, encodes, transcripts, labels, frames, audio
   stems, features, benchmark derivatives, and future derivatives.
2. **Participant block.** Every repeated recording for one participant,
   wearer, household, kitchen, team, or controlled-capture identity remains
   in one split. CaptainCook4D person-level supergroups and Assembly101
   participant blocks are mandatory.
3. **Production lineage.** Mirrors, excerpts, alternate edits, documentary
   episodes sharing components, embedded clips, and correlated production
   families remain grouped. NASA/JPL alternatives are not independent
   lineages; EPIC and HD-EPIC are not independent-lineage credit; Ego4D and
   Ego-Exo4D are not independent-lineage credit.

Participant-disjoint sources from one dataset may occupy different splits
only after participant, household, scene, kitchen, continuous-recording, and
derivative overlap checks pass. Such sources remain one dataset-lineage
stratum for diversity reporting.

### 3.2 Untouched means untouched

All test groups and test reserves are assigned **before** semantic review.
Before test unlock, permitted operations are limited to identity, rights,
privacy, lineage, deduplication, hashes, duration, streams, decoding,
synchronization, custody, retention, withdrawal, and deletion checks.

Before unlock, the following are prohibited:

- watching or listening for candidate discovery;
- opening captions, transcripts, narrations, action/sound/mistake labels,
  semantic timestamps, QA, or benchmark splits;
- ASR, OCR, AST, CLAP, CLIP, embedding search, LLM summaries, VLM inspection,
  retrieval scoring, or any model-guided mining;
- question, answer, claim, evidence, shortcut, modality, hop, or near/far
  authoring; and
- choosing test groups based on pilot shortages, model behavior, perceived
  difficulty, or candidate yield.

After the train/dev protocol is locked, a fresh test-only human team may
perform source-uniform authoring. **Model-guided test authoring and candidate mining remain prohibited**
permanently. No test model run may occur until all test H2 records and
intervention manifests are frozen.

## 6. Acquisition and admission gates

Every source must pass all five gate classes:

1. **Identity/acquisition:** pinned first-party release or item; exact object,
   asset, or session IDs; expected bytes and immutable identity; atomic
   restricted custody; no mirror or derivative counted separately.
2. **Rights/privacy:** affirmative acquisition, internal processing, and
   restricted human annotation authority; consent, withdrawal, retention,
   deletion, takedown, attribution, and least-privilege controls; component
   ledger for music, marks, faces, voices, partner/archive media, and
   embedded clips. Release remains separate.
3. **Duration:** one coherent wall-clock source from 1,200 through 5,400
   seconds. Do not sum synchronized views or concatenate unrelated takes.
4. **Audio/video:** decodable video and native synchronized audio; codec,
   channels, sample rate, nonempty decoded samples, stable source-time, no
   truncation, and beginning/middle/end plus stream-duration A/V deltas no
   greater than 0.25 seconds. Subtitles do not prove audio.
5. **Human semantic eligibility:** source-uniform visual, speech, and
   non-speech-audio prescreens as applicable; full-source recurrence and
   shortcut search; coherent multi-hop construction for far; concrete
   localizable source-world sound for A.

Music, score, applause, generic room noise, generic handling noise, coarse
audio labels, and visible/spoken duplicates do not earn A credit.

## 8. Human six-role review

All 90 authored candidates receive exactly six roles held by six distinct
people:

1. question/claim author or independent verifier;
2. blind full-source alternative-evidence finder;
3. validator A;
4. validator B;
5. independent adjudicator; and
6. blind post-adjudication saturation auditor.

That is **540 role assignments**. The staffing target is 18 unique
train/dev reviewers and 12 unique test-only reviewers, 30 total.

Source-level blindness is fail-closed:

- prescreeners cannot later be blind finder or saturation auditor on that
  source;
- finder and auditor may each hold that blind role for at most one family
  from a source;
- an adjudicator cannot have authored another family in the same source;
- nonblind exposure permanently disqualifies later blind roles on that
  source; and
- assignment or exposure mutation after packet freeze invalidates the
  affected packet.

H2 requires two validators, independent adjudication, blind full-source
alternative search, blind post-adjudication saturation audit, and all
intervention-v2/leakage prerequisites. The permitted completeness phrase is
“all accepted SESs discovered under the frozen saturation protocol.”

## 9. Prospective narrowing

Narrowing occurs before the affected test packets or model runs:

- **V+S+A:** if fewer than four train/dev sources yield a defensible V+S+A
  candidate, or fewer than two production-disjoint test groups remain
  eligible for V+S+A prospecting, reduce/remove the V+S+A target by addendum.
- **Far:** after all 54 train/dev candidates, if fewer than 18 far candidates
  or 12 far H2 survive, narrow the far claim and test target.
- **Yield:** if train/dev produces fewer than 36 H2 or fewer than 15 of 18
  groups contribute H2, repair the protocol and rerun excluded calibration.
  Do not mine test to compensate.
- **Test pool:** if fewer than 12 untouched groups, four capture formats, or
  the required independent lineages survive, keep test locked and use only
  the next untouched predeclared reserve.
- **Paper claims:** any modality, mechanism, domain, or capture-format claim
  with fewer than five untouched test groups is deleted or narrowed to
  source-bounded description.

## 11. Contamination ledger

Automatic invalidation applies to:

- any pre-unlock semantic or model exposure on test;

## 13. Go / Repair / No-Go

### Preferred GO

All are required: at least 60 H2, 30 contributing sources, 12 contributing
untouched test groups, 24/12/24 train/dev/test H2, no more than three H2 per
source, at least four capture formats, no more than 25% presentation-wrapped
sources, five untouched test sources for every paper-facing stratum claim,
and all rights, privacy, lineage, human-review, contamination, intervention,
leakage, runtime, competence, and ITT gates.

### Descriptive fallback

At least 60 H2, 20 sources, and eight untouched test groups under unchanged
construct and governance rules. Reporting is descriptive only and cannot
support causal/model-effect claims, controller superiority, population
ranking, or broad modality/mechanism generalization.

### Repair

Use `REPAIR` when unchanged reserves can close an unexposed failed slot,
train/dev authoring targets can be narrowed prospectively, or assignment and
packet defects can be corrected without touching test or weakening H2.

### No-Go

Use `NO_GO` when scale falls below 60/20/8; contamination has no untouched
replacement; source/participant/production leakage cannot be resolved;
rights/privacy/deletion obligations cannot be met; claims require forced
labels, pooled mechanisms, hidden labels, model-guided test mining, or
outcome-conditioned replacement; or the budget closes only by dropping one
of the six roles or saturation audit.
