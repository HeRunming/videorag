## Conditions attached to the engineering pass

The synthetic engineering pass must not be restated as:

- scientific validation of insufficiency reasoning;
- evidence that one of the four bundled policies is superior;
- readiness for natural media;
- certification of the media-derived index by the validator certificate;
- validation of adversarial isolation;
- permission to acquire or annotate named assets.

The following remain required before a real-content rehearsal can execute:

1. **Actual Gate A approvals.** Assign named rights/legal, acquisition/data,
   and privacy/ethics reviewers. Freeze affirmative item-level evidence and
   decisions for the exact rendition. No `unknown_hold` may remain for the
   intended action.
2. **Real-media adapter validation.** Replace or supplement procedural
   detectors with frozen natural-media perception and transcript provenance.
   Demonstrate rejection on corrupt, ambiguous, and inconsistent real inputs.
3. **Independent validation workflow.** Use genuinely independent people or
   separately implemented validation paths. Do not treat generated validator
   identities as evidence of human independence.
4. **Media certificate extension.** Bind the exact media-derived index,
   adapter versions/configuration, and build record into the certificate or a
   separate signed certificate.
5. **Metric completion.** Implement the missing metrics and denominators listed
   in the red-team report, including wall time/tokens, Claim-Response
   Consistency, per-hop results, search-policy features, recovery failure
   modes, strata, and missing-pair accounting.
6. **Untrusted-agent sandbox.** Remove repository/dataset filesystem access,
   network access, inherited credentials, `/proc` visibility, and uncontrolled
   subprocess/system-call capability. Add resource and timing controls.
7. **Normative restoration resolution.** Formally resolve the conflict between
   D-012/section 7 and the implemented common-endpoint
   `paired_restoration_gain`.

## Gate A status

**Gate A is closed.**

The rehearsal packets contain no named approvals and all substantive rights
decisions remain `unknown_hold`. This review does not alter those decisions and
does not approve any candidate. The only authorized next step is to request and
conduct human review of the named packets without downloading media.

## Final disposition

Keep `mvp/results/media_backed_v2/` as historical evidence. Use
`mvp/results/media_backed_v2_final/` as the post-review synthetic engineering
record. Proceed to human Gate A review preparation, but do not download,
annotate, broadly acquire, or scale.
