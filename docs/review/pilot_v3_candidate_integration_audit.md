- CaptainCook4D person×environment groups `16_20`, `15_29`, and `26_22`;

The matrix is structurally complete at the source/slot level and every slot has

lineage, a provisional unaccepted SES, missing-field inventory, rights/release
boundary, Gate-A blockers, and an inert Task145 intervention-v2 plan.

The decision is nevertheless **REPAIR**, not READY:

1. the source composition cannot honestly realize the frozen broad profile
   balance from current local evidence;
2. the 6/6 one/multi and 6/6 near/far targets cannot be reached without
   arbitrary composition or false labels;
3. all upstream substantive records are development-only, and their permanent
   Gate-A ineligibility is preserved;
4. trained-human Task153 prescreens, source-specific governance, six-role
   construct review, accepted SESs, and the Task145 causal/leakage interlock
   remain incomplete.

The honest matrix is therefore a **visual-procedure-heavy prospective matrix
with two speech controls**, not a balanced broad A/V pilot.

## 2. Inputs and audit method

The machine-readable matrix hash-binds the principal inputs. Source/index
evidence was used locally only. No network access, new media decoding, source
annotation file, benchmark action/recipe label, human identity, human
observation, or scientific episode was introduced.

### Selection rules applied

1. Prefer upstream `KEEP`/`KEEP_NARROWED` substantive proposals.
2. Use an upstream `REPAIR` only when it is the strongest honest second slot
   for that source and record the unresolved defect.
3. If a source lacks a second surviving proposal, author a narrowed slot only
   from already-recorded local source/index evidence.
4. Never reuse a rejected arbitrary pair as multi-hop.
5. Never turn AST, Whisper, CLIP, OpenCV, waveform, OCR, or caption routing
   output into human semantic evidence.
6. Never force modality, hop, or temporal labels to satisfy a quota.
7. Preserve every record as P-tier candidate planning with zero accepted SES.

## 9. Human protocol boundary

distinct identities per family:

1. question/claim author or verifier;
2. blind alternative-evidence finder;
3. validator A;
4. validator B;
5. adjudicator;
6. blind post-adjudication saturation auditor.

Before packet distribution, the coordinator must:

- recruit and train at least eight unique reviewers across the 12-family pilot;
- complete two excluded calibrations;
- record prior source-group exposure;
- freeze all assignments before distribution;
- enforce source-group blind-role and adjudicator/author conflicts;
- hash-chain every review exposure;
- ensure any Task153 prescreener or author is excluded from blind finder and
  saturation roles for that source group.

No family can receive H1 or H2 from this matrix. H2 additionally requires
passing adjudication, a later independent full-source saturation audit, no new
sufficient route, and no unresolved rights/privacy/safety hold.
